05 / MATERIALS & CARE

Choose well.
Live with it.

A few useful notes for choosing timber and keeping a finished piece beautiful.

Material guide

Oak, walnut or ash?

Oak brings a light, structured grain. Walnut is deeper and more dramatic. Ash is pale, tactile and wonderfully resilient.

Care guide

Keep the surface honest.

Dust with a soft cloth, wipe spills quickly, and refresh an oil finish when the timber begins to feel dry.

Planning guide

Measure twice.

Photograph the room, note doorways and leave space for movement. These details make a beautiful piece work harder.
